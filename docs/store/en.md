## Full description (1 763 chars) — 1830 chars

```
A blocky sandbox written from scratch in TypeScript and WebGL2, playable on phones and desktop browsers.

■ World
Infinite terrain, caves, ore veins and biomes: plains, forest, desert, snowy tundra, jungle, swamp, deep ocean… Hills and sea floors are generated from real height data, and there is no wall at the edge.
Over 20 explorable structures: villages, abandoned mineshafts, strongholds, dungeons, ocean monuments, woodland mansions, bastion remnants, end cities. The same seed always gives the same world.

■ Gameplay
· Survival: hunger, health, drops, respawn points and four difficulties — on Peaceful no hostile mobs spawn
· Creative: flight, unlimited blocks, instant break, plus a full building toolkit (select / fill / copy / paste / mirror / undo / templates)
· 200+ crafting recipes: crafting table, furnace, smoker, blast furnace, campfire, stonecutter, smithing table, brewing stand, loom, cartography table, anvil, grindstone, enchanting table
· 70+ mobs, including villager trading, taming and riding
· 36 commands with a graphical help panel (pick parameters, insert straight into chat)
· Minigames and a first-run tutorial, so nobody starts out lost

■ Feel
Two control schemes — touch and keyboard/mouse — landscape fullscreen, adjustable button size and sensitivity, double-tap to sneak, hold to mine.
Interface in six languages: 简体中文, English, Deutsch, Русский, Français, Português (Brasil).

■ About
This is an unofficial fan remake, not affiliated with Mojang or Microsoft, and it contains no official assets.
Every texture and sound is generated by code at runtime. Saves live on your own device (browser local storage) and are never uploaded.

■ Notes
· The first launch generates a world and may take a few seconds on low-end devices
· Landscape is recommended; add to home screen to launch fullscreen
```
